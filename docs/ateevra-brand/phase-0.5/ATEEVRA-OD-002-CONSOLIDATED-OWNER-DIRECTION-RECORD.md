1. **OD-003 (Catalog & Legal Metrology):** PDP specifications will be architected to support two statutory labeling flows: (a) "Manufactured by [Contract Atelier] for ATEEVRA" for bespoke apparel, and (b) "Manufactured by [Specialist Vendor] and Packed/Marketed by ATEEVRA" for finished accessories, fully compliant with Rule 6(10) of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.
2. **OD-022 (Supplier Qualification):** Establishes two distinct vetting tracks under `Gate G-QA` and `Gate G-SUP`: Track 1 for master tailoring ateliers (cutting precision, hand-stitching, pattern conformity), and Track 2 for finished-goods manufacturers (ethical compliance, material certificates, finished quality).
3. **OD-026 (Landed Cost BOM Accounting):** Standardizes accounting policies under Ind AS 2: standard absorption costing for bespoke contract manufacturing (fabric + CMT + trims + inbound freight) alongside purchase invoice capitalization for finished accessories.
4. **OD-023 & OD-027 (Milestone Financing & Working Capital):** Models a blended cash-flow profile combining 30/40/30 milestone advances for bespoke garments with standard commercial credit / invoice payment terms for finished products.
