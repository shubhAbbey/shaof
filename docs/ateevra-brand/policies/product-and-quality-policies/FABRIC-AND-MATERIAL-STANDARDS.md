## 1. Ethical Sourcing and Fiber Integrity

ATEEVRA exclusively partners with certified spinning mills, heritage weaving looms, and tanneries that adhere to the highest global benchmarks of ecological stewardship and fiber authenticity.

1. **Natural Fiber Supremacy:** A minimum of 90% of all apparel collections must be composed of natural or certified cellulose fibers (Grade-A Mulberry Silk, Belgian Flax Linen, Super 120s–150s Merino Wool, Organic Pima Cotton).
2. **Synthetic Fiber Restrictions:** Synthetic polymers (elastane, recycled polyester) are permitted solely when structurally necessary (e.g., technical lining stability, stretch recovery in slim tailoring), capped at $\le 10\%$ of total fabric composition.
3. **Traceability & Certification:** All raw material lots must be backed by GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard), RWS (Responsible Wool Standard), or OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certifications.

---

## 2. Lab Testing & Rejection Thresholds

Every fabric consignment delivered to contract ateliers must satisfy laboratory testing prior to cut-and-sew authorization:
- **Dimensional Shrinkage:** Maximum allowable shrinkage $\le 2.0\%$ (ISO 6330).
- **Color Fastness to Rubbing:** Dry crocking Grade 4.5, Wet crocking Grade 4.0 minimum (ISO 105-X12).
- **Tensile & Tear Resistance:** Zero yarn slippage at seam stress points under 180N load.
