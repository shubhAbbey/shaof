## 1. Background & Context
In digital luxury commerce, unboxing is the defining physical brand moment. Standard plastic poly mailers and flimsy brown cardboard boxes convey cheapness and destroy client enchantment.

## 2. Problem Statement
What physical packaging specifications, aesthetic standards, and security measures should ATEEVRA deploy for client deliveries?

## 3. Decision Alternatives Considered

### Option A: Three-Tier Luxury Presentation Box with Discreet Kraft Outer Shipper (RECOMMENDED)
- **Description:** Garments packaged in magnetic-closure rigid boxes (1100–1400 GSM) with debossed foil heraldry, organic cotton dustbags, monogrammed tissue paper, hand-poured wax seal, and personalized welcome cards; encased in discreet, unbranded 5-ply Kraft outer shippers with tamper-evident fiberglass tape.
- **Pros:** Unmatched luxury unboxing experience; social-media shareability; unbranded outer carton prevents transit pilferage.
- **Cons:** Higher packaging cost (₹180 – ₹280 per unit); higher volumetric parcel weight.

### Option B: Standard Polyethylene Courier Mailer Bags
- **Description:** Sealing garments in standard flexible plastic courier flyers.
- **Pros:** Extremely low cost (₹8 per bag); negligible volumetric weight.
- **Cons:** Wrinkled garments upon arrival; cheap perception; antithetical to luxury brand positioning.

### Option C: Branded Exterior Printed Shippers
- **Description:** Outer shipping boxes boldly emblazoned with "ATEEVRA LUXURY COUTURE".
- **Pros:** High brand visibility on street and in transit.
- **Cons:** Dramatically increases courier theft and pilferage in transit.

---

## 4. Recommended Option & Justification
**Option A** is strongly recommended. Unboxing is an integral component of the product itself in luxury fashion, while discreet outer packaging protects high-ticket inventory in transit.
