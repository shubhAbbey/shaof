# ATEEVRA — Luxury Concierge Return, Exchange & Restocking Policy

**Document ID:** `ATEEVRA-POL-RET-001`  
**Classification:** Customer Return, Exchange & Quality Assurance Policy  
**Status:** PROPOSED (Pending Owner Decision)

---

## 1. Return Window and Concierge Intake

1. **7-Day Concierge Window:** Clients may request a return or size exchange within **seven (7) calendar days** of verified physical delivery.
2. **Concierge-Assisted Intake:** Returns and exchanges are initiated seamlessly via our official WhatsApp Concierge or Account Portal. No tedious self-service dispute forms or waiting queues.
3. **Simultaneous Size Swap:** If an exchange is requested for fit, ATEEVRA cross-ships the new size immediately while dispatching a courier to collect the original item, eliminating client downtime.

---

## 2. Garment Return Condition Standards

To preserve luxury hygiene and protect subsequent clients, returned garments must meet strict condition criteria:
1. **Security Tag Intact:** The black grosgrain security ribbon with the serialized tamper-evident seal (`"DO NOT REMOVE"`) must remain completely unbroken and attached to the garment.
2. **Pristine Unworn State:** Garments must be free of perfume, makeup stains, body odor, deodorant marks, alterations, or fabric snags.
3. **Original Accoutrements:** Garments must be returned with their original branded wooden hanger, cotton dustbag, and presentation packaging.

---

## 3. Refund Execution and Timelines

1. **Refund Method:** Approved refunds are remitted 100% to the original payment instrument (credit card, bank account, UPI) within **48 hours** of warehouse inspection pass.
2. **Zero Restocking Fees:** ATEEVRA does not deduct reverse shipping charges or restocking fees for legitimate returns meeting condition criteria.
