### 2.2 Workstream 2: Professional Validation Gateway
- **Scope:** Commissioning five licensed external specialist authorities to validate high-risk statutory, fiscal, legal, quality, and technical boundaries.
- **Specialist Gates:**
  1. **Gate G-CA (Statutory Chartered Accountant):** Validating Section 31 tax invoice issuance, Section 52 TCS inapplicability, HSN ITC asset recognition, gateway MDR accounting, and Ind AS 2 inventory costing.
  2. **Gate G-LG (Corporate & IP Legal Counsel):** Confirming warehouse legal status under The Factories Act, 1948 vs. Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1961; Legal Metrology PDP compliance; Terms of Sale; and DPDP Act 2023 consent architecture.
  3. **Gate G-QA (Accredited Textile Testing Laboratory):** Confirming ISO 2859-1:1999 Table 1/2-A sampling plan, AQL 2.5 defect bounds, and fabric ozone sanitization safety.
  4. **Gate G-LO (Logistics & Supply Chain Specialist):** Auditing carrier OTP delivery integration and high-value transit liability agreements.
  5. **Gate G-SC (Cybersecurity Auditor):** Auditing Next.js 14 / Medusa admin BFF security, hardware TOTP enforcement, and dual-authorization approval barriers.
- **Deliverable Referenced:** [`ATEEVRA-PROFESSIONAL-ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md`](file:///C:/Users/Shubham/OneDrive/Desktop/ecom_mvp/docs/ateevra-brand/phase-0.5/ATEEVRA-PROFESSIONAL-ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md).
