## 1. Brand Equity & Price Integrity Principles

ATEEVRA adheres to the tenets of **Quiet Luxury**. Excessive discounting, frantic countdown timers, sitewide flash sales, and slash-price marketing fundamentally destroy luxury brand equity and client trust.

---

## 2. Promotional Restrictions & Permitted Channels

1. **Prohibition of Sitewide Discounts:** Sitewide percentage-off sales (e.g., "50% Off Everything") are permanently prohibited across all public channels.
2. **Private VIP Seasonal Previews:** Private, password-protected previews are permitted up to twice per year for verified Circle Members, restricted to off-season archives, with discounts capped at a maximum of 25%.
3. **No Marketplace Price Parity Conflicts:** ATEEVRA does not distribute through third-party multi-vendor marketplaces, eliminating any risk of cross-platform unauthorized discounting.
4. **Floor Price Guardrail:** Medusa's pricing rule engine automatically invalidates and rejects any order where net realization breaches the unit Contribution Protection Floor Price ($\text{Landed Cost} + \text{Logistics} + \text{Taxes}$).
