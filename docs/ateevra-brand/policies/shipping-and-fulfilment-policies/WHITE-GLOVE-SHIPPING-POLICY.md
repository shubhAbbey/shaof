## 1. Fulfilment Architecture and Carrier Selection

ATEEVRA exclusively partners with premier express air carriers (Blue Dart Apex Priority, Delhivery Air Express, DHL Express Worldwide) to ensure rapid, secure, and pristine delivery.

1. **Complimentary Air Shipping:** Complimentary priority air shipping is extended on all domestic orders with a net invoice value exceeding **₹3,500**. For orders below this threshold, a flat delivery fee of ₹150 is applied.
2. **Dispatch SLAs:** Orders placed before 14:00 IST are dispatched on the same business day; orders placed after 14:00 IST are dispatched within 24 hours.
3. **Discreet Outer Packaging:** To prevent cargo pilferage, outer corrugated shipping boxes feature zero external luxury brand markings or value indicators.

---

## 2. High-Value Delivery Verification

1. **OTP Verification:** Shipments with an invoice value exceeding ₹10,000 mandate customer verification via a dynamic 6-digit OTP transmitted via SMS/WhatsApp prior to physical handover.
2. **Damage in Transit:** If an outer shipping container arrives compromised, the customer is entitled to an immediate replacement dispatched within 4 hours, without waiting for carrier damage claims resolution.
