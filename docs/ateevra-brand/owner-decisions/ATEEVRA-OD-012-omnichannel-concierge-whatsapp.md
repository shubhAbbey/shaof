## 1. Background & Context
Luxury clients expect real-time personal advisory rather than impersonal ticketing systems, chatbot loops, or lengthy email waiting times. In India and key international luxury markets, WhatsApp is the preferred conversational platform.

## 2. Problem Statement
How should ATEEVRA architect its primary customer support channels, response SLAs, and human client advisor staffing?

## 3. Decision Alternatives Considered

### Option A: Official WhatsApp Business Cloud API with 5-Minute Human SLA (RECOMMENDED)
- **Description:** Verified WhatsApp green-badge concierge backed by dedicated client advisors (09:00 to 21:00 IST); 5-minute initial human response SLA; integrated with CRM and Medusa order lookup; phone trunk for complex inquiries.
- **Pros:** Conversational elegance; instant fit guidance and lookbook sharing; high customer satisfaction; drives direct repeat orders.
- **Cons:** Requires dedicated, well-trained luxury advisors.

### Option B: AI-Only Chatbot with Email Escalation
- **Description:** Deploying an automated bot handling 100% of queries with generic macro answers.
- **Pros:** Lowest labor cost.
- **Cons:** Impersonal, frustrating chatbot loops; severely degrades luxury positioning.

---

## 4. Recommended Option & Justification
**Option A** is recommended. Personal concierge advisory is a hallmark of luxury commerce, transforming customer support from a cost center into a high-converting client retention engine.
