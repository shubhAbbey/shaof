## 1. Background & Context
Transitioning to a first-party luxury fashion brand requires rigorous coordination across technology, physical warehousing, legal compliance, tax accounting, and marketing. Launching without structured quality gates risks software failures, tax audits, or poor customer reception.

## 2. Problem Statement
What rollout methodology, phased milestone sequence, and third-party professional validation sign-off gates should govern the brand debut?

## 3. Decision Alternatives Considered

### Option A: 16-Week 4-Phase Delivery Plan with 5 Independent Professional Sign-Off Gates (RECOMMENDED)
- **Description:** Phase 1 (Foundation & Decoupling, W1–4); Phase 2 (Operations, Logistics & Tax Invoicing, W5–8); Phase 3 (Luxury Storefront & Concierge CX, W9–12); Phase 4 (Pilot Launch & VIP Capsule Debut, W13–16); mandatory independent sign-offs from Chartered Accountant, Legal Counsel, Fashion QC Specialist, Logistics Advisor, and Ethical Security Auditor.
- **Pros:** Comprehensive risk mitigation; zero codebase regressions; institutional credibility; smooth escalation from private VIP capsule to public debut.
- **Cons:** Requires strict adherence to a 16-week delivery discipline.

### Option B: Immediate "Big Bang" Public Launch (4-Week Compressed Timeline)
- **Description:** Deploying code modifications, opening storefront, and announcing public sales within 30 days without independent audits or pilot testing.
- **Pros:** Fast time-to-market.
- **Cons:** Catastrophic failure probability: unverified tax invoices, broken courier integrations, pick-pack errors, and brand-destroying delivery delays.

---

## 4. Recommended Option & Justification
**Option A** is the only responsible path. Luxury excellence requires meticulous preparation, independent verification, and disciplined execution.
