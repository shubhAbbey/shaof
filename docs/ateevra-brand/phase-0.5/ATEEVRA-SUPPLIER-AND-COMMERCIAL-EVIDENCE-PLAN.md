## 1. Executive Summary & Commercial Verification Mandate

During Phase BR-01, the financial model established that all Bill of Materials (BOM) labor rates, fabric costs, Input Tax Credit (ITC) percentages, packaging quotes, and freight rates were **`MODEL ASSUMPTIONS`** due to the absence of signed commercial agreements in the monorepo.

Phase 0.5 establishes a structured procurement and vendor engagement process to convert every modeled financial assumption into **binding, verified commercial evidence**.

Prior to Phase 1 entry, physical evidence files will be collected, validated against target unit economic limits, and logged into the commercial evidence repository.

## 3. Detailed Intake Protocols & Evidence Specifications

### 3.1 Stream 1: B2B Atelier & CMT Manufacturing Evidence
- **Objective:** Secure binding cut-make-trim (CMT) labor quotations from three qualified export-grade garment manufacturers located in Bengaluru, Tirupur, or Mumbai.
- **Initial Capsule Scope:**
  1. **Structured Tailored Blazer (Menswear/Womenswear):** Half-canvas construction, cupro lining, AMF edge pick-stitching. Target Model CMT: **₹1,350.00**.
  2. **Tailored Wool Trousers:** Extended waistband, curtain lining, horn buttons. Target Model CMT: **₹750.00**.
  3. **Mulberry Silk Long-Sleeve Shirt:** French seams, mother-of-pearl buttons. Target Model CMT: **₹550.00**.
- **Required Tangible Evidence:**
  - Formal signed quotation sheets on atelier letterhead specifying price breaks for 250, 500, and 1,000 units.
  - Completed Factory Ethical & Technical Audit Checklist (evaluating machinery, lighting, fire safety, minimum wage compliance).
  - Draft Master Service Agreement (MSA) containing explicit **30/40/30 milestone payment terms** under [`ATEEVRA-OD-023`](file:///C:/Users/Shubham/OneDrive/Desktop/ecom_mvp/docs/ateevra-brand/phase-0.5/ATEEVRA-OWNER-DECISION-REVIEW-AGENDA.md#43-ateevra-od-023-purchase-order-milestone-financing--advance-payment-terms).

### 3.2 Stream 2: Raw Material Mills & Luxury Trims Evidence
- **Objective:** Establish direct mill supply relationships for primary shell fabrics and certified trims, obtaining exact pro-forma tax invoices to validate statutory HSN codes and Input Tax Credit (ITC) eligibility.
- **Material Specifications & Cost Targets:**
  1. **100% Mulberry Silk Charmeuse (19 momme, 140cm width):** Target: **₹1,400.00 / meter** (HSN 5007, 5% GST).
  2. **100% Superfine Worsted Merino Wool (Super 120s, 280 GSM):** Target: **₹1,050.00 / meter** (HSN 5111, 5% GST).
  3. **100% Cupro Bemberg Lining (80 GSM):** Target: **₹280.00 / meter** (HSN 5408, 5% GST).
  4. **Genuine Buffalo Horn & Electroplated Brass Buttons:** Target: **₹180.00 / garment set** (HSN 9606, 18% GST).
  5. **YKK Excella High-Shine Luxury Zippers:** Target: **₹90.00 / unit** (HSN 9607, 18% GST).
- **Required Tangible Evidence:**
  - Mill fabric sample swatches with laboratory composition certificates (yarn count, shrinkage, colorfastness).
  - Supplier pro-forma tax invoices displaying valid GSTIN, HSN classification, and itemized IGST/CGST rates.
  - Formal submission to Chartered Accountant (`Gate G-CA`) to confirm full GSTR-2B ITC asset recognition.

### 3.3 Stream 3: Three-Tier Luxury Packaging Evidence
- **Objective:** Secure manufacturing quotes and physical prototype samples for the bespoke 3-tier unboxing experience, ensuring total landed packaging cost does not exceed the model cap of **₹220.00 per order**.
- **Packaging Component Breakdown:**
  1. **Tier 1 (Outer Transit Shipper):** 3-ply kraft corrugated box, plain unbranded exterior, tamper-evident reinforced water-activated tape. Target: **₹35.00**.
  2. **Tier 2 (Luxury Rigid Presentation Box):** 1,200 GSM recycled rigid greyboard wrapped in soft-touch matte paper, concealed magnetic closure, debossed gold-foil logo. Target: **₹120.00**.
  3. **Tier 3 (Tactile Presentation & Accessories):** Custom printed 24 GSM FSC-certified tissue paper, double-faced satin ribbon, branded wax seal, personalized handwritten customer welcome note. Target: **₹35.00**.
  4. **Breathable Garment Bag:** 100 GSM non-woven water-repellent fabric carrier with metallic zipper (for tailored blazers). Target: **₹30.00**.
- **Required Tangible Evidence:**
  - Formal quotation matrix from packaging manufacturers (e.g., in Mumbai, Noida, or Bengaluru) showing tiered pricing for 1,000, 2,500, and 5,000 unit production runs.
  - Physical unboxing sample approved by Luxury Brand Director.

### 3.4 Stream 4: Priority Air Logistics & Warehousing Evidence
- **Objective:** Finalize binding freight agreements for guaranteed 24–48h express delivery and confirm fulfillment facility physical readiness.
- **Logistics Specifications:**
  - Forward Priority Air Delivery: Metro 24–36h, Non-Metro 48h. Target Net Freight: **₹165.00 / 1kg volumetric package**.
  - Reverse Return / Size Swap Courier: Dedicated reverse pickup with doorstep inspection. Target Provision: **₹150.00 / return**.
  - Mandatory SMS Delivery OTP Integration: Carrier webhook API documentation verifying dynamic OTP handover.
- **Central Warehouse WH01 (Bengaluru):**
  - Commercial lease agreement or signed Letter of Intent (LOI) for 8,500 sq ft facility in Peenya / Electronic City / Whitefield industrial logistics corridors.
  - Engineering quotations for negative-pressure ozone sanitization room and commercial heavy-duty steam-ironing tables.
- **Required Tangible Evidence:**
  - Executed Enterprise Master Services Agreement with **Blue Dart Express Ltd.** or **Delhivery Ltd.**
  - Signed warehouse facility lease LOI with architectural floor plan.

### 3.5 Stream 5: Payment Gateway Enterprise Fee Schedule Evidence
- **Objective:** Secure executed merchant agreement from payment aggregator (Razorpay, PayU, or Cashfree) to eliminate fee basis uncertainty.
- **Required Evidence:**
  - Executed commercial agreement specifying:
    1. Base MDR percentage (2.0% on Gross GMV vs. Net Revenue).
    2. Applicable payment methods (UPI, Credit Cards, Net Banking, EMI).
    3. Monthly GST tax invoice format for claiming GSTR-2B input tax credit on the 18% GST assessed on banking fees.
