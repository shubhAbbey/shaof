## 1. Background & Context
Re-selling a worn or compromised luxury garment destroys brand credibility immediately. Returned garments must undergo rigorous triage before being permitted back into active stock.

## 2. Problem Statement
How should ATEEVRA inspect, sanitize, refurbish, and re-stock returned inventory without compromising luxury quality or violating hygiene standards?

## 3. Decision Alternatives Considered

### Option A: 5-Stage Triage, Ozone Sanitation Cabinet, Fresh Luxury Packaging (RECOMMENDED)
- **Description:** Unboxing under 4K video surveillance; security tag check; fabric/odor inspection; ozone sanitation cabinet treatment; professional steam pressing; re-boxing with fresh tissue paper; re-entry to Medusa as `RESTOCKED_GRADE_A`.
- **Pros:** 100% guarantee that restocked inventory is indistinguishable from brand new; video audit protects against fraudulent claims; ozone neutralizes odors without harsh chemicals.
- **Cons:** Equipment capex for ozone cabinet and tension steaming; operational processing labor (~₹150 per unit).

### Option B: Immediate Return to Bin Without Treatment
- **Description:** Folding returned garments and placing them directly back on warehouse shelves.
- **Pros:** Lowest processing cost and fastest inventory replenishment.
- **Cons:** High risk of sending a wrinkled, perfume-stained, or makeup-marked garment to the next VIP customer; catastrophic brand damage.

---

## 4. Recommended Option & Justification
**Option A** is strongly recommended. Luxury standards require that every parcel unboxed by a client smells and feels immaculate.
