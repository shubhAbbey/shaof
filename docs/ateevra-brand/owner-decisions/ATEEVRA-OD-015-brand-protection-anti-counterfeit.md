## 1. Background & Context
Premium and luxury fashion brands are highly vulnerable to counterfeit copies, grey-market diversion, and unauthorized distributor reselling, which dilute brand prestige and erode pricing power.

## 2. Problem Statement
What technical authentication measures, trademark monitoring, and legal enforcement strategies should ATEEVRA institute to safeguard intellectual property?

## 3. Decision Alternatives Considered

### Option A: Embedded Holographic Serialization & Proactive Anti-Counterfeit Monitoring (RECOMMENDED)
- **Description:** Every garment incorporates a serialized cryptographic QR/RFID label linked to an internal authenticity registry; registered trademarks across apparel, leather, and digital commerce; automated crawling of marketplaces and social media for counterfeit takedowns.
- **Pros:** Guarantees garment authenticity for clients; instant takedown of unauthorized grey-market listings; protects long-term brand valuation.
- **Cons:** Serialization label production cost (~₹25 per unit); ongoing trademark surveillance subscription.

### Option B: Passive Brand Protection (Basic Trademark Registration Only)
- **Description:** Registering the trademark name without active market enforcement or serialized security tags.
- **Pros:** Lower ongoing operational cost.
- **Cons:** High vulnerability to knock-offs and grey-market leaks; difficult to prove provenance in return fraud disputes.

---

## 4. Recommended Option & Justification
**Option A** is recommended. Brand equity is the core enterprise asset of a luxury fashion house and must be defended rigorously.
