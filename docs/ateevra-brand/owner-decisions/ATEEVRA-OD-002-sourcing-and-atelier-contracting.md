## 1. Background & Context
ATEEVRA requires high-quality garment fabrication matching international luxury houses. As a first-party brand, ATEEVRA must decide whether to own and operate physical manufacturing factories, contract specialized third-party ateliers under strict specifications (CMT/FOB), or procure white-label stock.

## 2. Problem Statement
What manufacturing and sourcing architecture should ATEEVRA adopt to guarantee sartorial perfection, flexibility, and capital efficiency?

## 3. Decision Alternatives Considered

### Option A: Certified Contract Atelier B2B Manufacturing (FOB / CMT) (RECOMMENDED)
- **Description:** ATEEVRA designs collections in-house, specifies tech packs and fabrics, and contracts certified luxury ateliers in India and Europe on a Purchase Order (PO) basis.
- **Pros:** High flexibility; zero factory capex; access to master artisans specializing in specific garments (e.g., suiting in Bengaluru/Delhi, silk in Como/Bengaluru, leather in Tuscany/Chennai).
- **Cons:** Requires rigorous supplier audits, AQL 2.5 QC gates, and advance working capital.
- **Financial Impact:** Predictable unit COGS; standard milestone financing (30/40/30).

### Option B: Vertical Integration (Company-Owned Atelier/Factory)
- **Description:** ATEEVRA establishes and operates its own dedicated garment manufacturing facility.
- **Pros:** 100% operational custody over every sewing station; zero supplier margin markup.
- **Cons:** Massive initial capital expenditure (machinery, factory leases); fixed labor overhead; slower ramp-up.
- **Financial Impact:** High fixed burn rate; severe negative drag on early EBITDA.

### Option C: Off-the-Shelf White-Label Rebranding
- **Description:** Purchasing ready-made garments from wholesale aggregators and attaching ATEEVRA labels.
- **Pros:** Minimal upfront lead time and lowest inventory cost.
- **Cons:** Destroys luxury brand credibility; inferior fabrics and generic fits; no design exclusivity.

---

## 4. Recommended Option & Justification
**Option A (Certified Contract Atelier B2B Manufacturing)** is recommended. Global leaders like Ralph Lauren, Hugo Boss, and Theory utilize specialized manufacturing ateliers while retaining 100% brand, pattern, and design ownership.
