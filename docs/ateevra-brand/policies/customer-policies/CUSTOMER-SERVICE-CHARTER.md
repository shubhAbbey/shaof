## 1. The ATEEVRA Service Creed

ATEEVRA is committed to providing an exceptional standard of client care. Our customer service operates as a bespoke private concierge, ensuring dignity, discretion, and prompt resolution at every point of contact.

---

## 2. Concierge Service Level Commitments

1. **Human Response Standard:** Every message received on the official WhatsApp Concierge channel receives a direct human response from a trained Client Advisor within **5 minutes** during operational hours (09:00 to 21:00 IST).
2. **Dedicated Styling Consultation:** Clients are entitled to complimentary virtual styling appointments to review fabric textures, color harmonies, and wardrobe integration.
3. **Escalation Guarantee:** If an issue cannot be resolved at the primary advisor level, it is escalated directly to the Concierge Team Lead within 2 hours, and to the Brand Director within 6 hours.

---

## 3. Statutory Grievance Redressal Mechanism

In compliance with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020:
- **Grievance Officer:** Designated Officer, ATEEVRA Luxury Enterprises Pvt Ltd.
- **Email:** `grievance@ateevra.com` | **Direct Trunk:** `+91 80 4910 ATEE`
- **Acknowledgment SLA:** Within 48 hours of ticket creation.
- **Redressal SLA:** Formal resolution within one calendar month.
