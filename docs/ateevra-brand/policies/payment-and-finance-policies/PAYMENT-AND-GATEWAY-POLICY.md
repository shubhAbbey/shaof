## 1. Authorized Payment Instruments

To provide security and convenience to high-net-worth clients, ATEEVRA supports enterprise-grade digital payment channels:
- **Major Domestic & International Credit Cards:** Visa Infinite, Mastercard World Elite, American Express Centurion/Platinum, Diners Club.
- **Unified Payments Interface (UPI):** Real-time UPI Intent & QR (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) with zero surcharge.
- **Net Banking:** Instant settlement across 50+ scheduled commercial banks in India.
- **International Cards & Currencies:** Multi-currency capture (USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD) with automated currency conversion via Stripe / Razorpay.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Severely restricted. COD is permitted only on orders between ₹2,500 and ₹15,000, subject to automated pre-dispatch phone verification. Orders $> ₹15,000$ mandate 100% prepaid settlement.

---

## 2. Gateway Fees and Merchant Discount Rate (MDR)

1. **No Customer Surcharge:** ATEEVRA absorbs 100% of payment gateway processing fees (MDR). Customers are never charged convenience fees or card processing surcharges at checkout.
2. **Settlement and Reconciliation:** All payment inflows settle directly into ATEEVRA's designated corporate current account on a T+1 business day cadence, reconciled against Medusa order records via daily automated bank ledger feeds.
