## 3. Formal Sign-Off Protocol

1. **Independent Evaluation:** Each specialist conducts an unhindered technical, financial, or legal assessment against statutory standards.
2. **Exception Remediation:** Any findings marked as `HIGH_RISK` or `BLOCKER` must be resolved by engineering/operations within 10 business days.
3. **Owner Executive Ratification:** The Project Owner reviews the certified validation letters before issuing the commercial launch order.
