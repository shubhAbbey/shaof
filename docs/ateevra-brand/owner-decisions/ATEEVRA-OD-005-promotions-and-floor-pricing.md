## 1. Background & Context
Mass-market discount tactics (flash sales, countdown timers, aggressive coupon popups) alienate high-net-worth luxury consumers and permanently damage brand prestige.

## 2. Problem Statement
How should ATEEVRA manage off-season inventory clearance and promotional incentives without diluting its brand equity?

## 3. Decision Alternatives Considered

### Option A: Strict Promotional Discipline with Private VIP Previews (RECOMMENDED)
- **Description:** Zero public sitewide sales. Off-season archive pieces are offered exclusively via password-protected, private VIP previews capped at 20%–25% off, protected by automated Floor Price guardrails.
- **Pros:** Preserves full brand equity; rewards high-value returning clients; automated engine prevents selling below contribution breakeven.
- **Cons:** Slower clearance velocity for aging inventory.

### Option B: Seasonal Public End-of-Season Sales (EOSS)
- **Description:** Public sitewide 40%–50% discount banners twice a year.
- **Pros:** Fast liquidation of slow-moving inventory.
- **Cons:** Conditions customers to delay purchases until sales; damages full-price luxury conversion.

### Option C: Complete Liquidation to Off-Price Grey Market Outlets
- **Description:** Selling unsold inventory to third-party discount aggregators.
- **Pros:** Immediate bulk cash recovery.
- **Cons:** High risk of brand erosion and unauthorized digital listings.

---

## 4. Recommended Option & Justification
**Option A** is recommended. The world's top luxury brands maintain strict promotional discipline to foster exclusivity and long-term client loyalty.
