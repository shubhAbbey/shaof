## 3. Deferrable Commitment Arithmetic & Packaging Adjustment Reconciliation

### 3.1 Correction of Deferrable Commitments Sum (₹10.85L vs. ₹10.82L)
The report previously cited:
> *"over ₹10.82 Lakhs (56.3%) is completely discretionary and deferrable."*

The exact mathematical sum of the four discretionary commitments is:
- Drop 2 Luxury Mill Fabric Reorders: **₹2,37,500.00**
- Incremental Performance Marketing (Months 2–3): **₹1,50,000.00**
- Digital Software Infrastructure Enhancements: **₹2,72,500.00**
- Ongoing Statutory Legal & Tax Retainers: **₹4,25,000.00**

$$\mathbf{₹2,37,500.00 + ₹1,50,000.00 + ₹2,72,500.00 + ₹4,25,000.00 = ₹10,85,000.00 \text{ (₹10.85 Lakhs)}}.$$

$$\text{Percentage of Incremental Commitments} = \frac{₹10,85,000.00}{₹19,22,080.00} = \mathbf{56.449\% \approx 56.45\%}.$$

- **Reason for Previous Figure:** The ₹10.82L figure was an arithmetic transcription slip where ₹3,000.00 was deducted. The authoritative corrected figure is **₹10,85,000.00 (₹10.85 Lakhs / 56.45%)**.

### 3.2 Reconciliation of Packaging & Buffer Adjustments (+₹0.6708L vs. -₹0.8292L)
The review noted two seemingly conflicting adjustments:
1. **The +₹0.6708L (+₹67,080.00) Adjustment in Ceiling Bridge Additions:**
   $$\text{Total Incremental Ceiling Bridge} = \mathbf{₹19,22,080.00}$$
   $$\text{Sum of 5 Explicit Commitments } (₹7.70\text{L M3} + ₹10.85\text{L Deferrable}) = \mathbf{₹18,55,000.00}$$
   $$\text{Residual Bridge Adjustment} = ₹19,22,080.00 - ₹18,55,000.00 = \mathbf{+₹67,080.00 \text{ (+₹0.6708 Lakhs)}}.$$
   *Source & Accounting Nature:* This represents the net balancing adjustment between:
   - Packaging Net PO variance: -₹19,800.00 (Initial pool had gross cash ₹1,29,800 vs. ceiling net PO of ₹1,10,000).
   - Unallocated Contingency reallocation: +₹86,880.00 (Residual liquid contingency absorbed into commitment ceiling).
   - $+₹86,880.00 - ₹19,800.00 = \mathbf{+₹67,080.00}$.

2. **The -₹0.8292L (-₹82,920.00 / -₹0.83L) Adjustment in Timeline Omitted Commitments:**
   In `ATEEVRA-120-DAY-CASH-FLOW-VALIDATION.md`, omitted commitments totaled **₹8,52,080.00**.
   The three primary unspent items totaled:
   $$₹2,37,500 \text{ (Fabric)} + ₹2,72,500 \text{ (Tech)} + ₹4,25,000 \text{ (Retainers)} = \mathbf{₹9,35,000.00}.$$
   $$\text{Difference} = ₹8,52,080.00 - ₹9,35,000.00 = \mathbf{-₹82,920.00 \text{ (-₹0.8292 Lakhs)}}.$$
   *The Exact Link Between Both Numbers:*
   In the timeline table, the author disbursed **₹3,00,000.00** for marketing instead of the **₹1,50,000.00** ceiling addition (+₹1,50,000.00 marketing over-allocation in the timeline).
   $$+₹67,080.00 \text{ (Ceiling Residual)} - ₹1,50,000.00 \text{ (Marketing Timing Swing)} = \mathbf{-₹82,920.00 \text{ (-₹0.83L)}}!$$

- **Effect on the ₹55.70L Ceiling:** **ZERO EFFECT**. The ceiling remains strictly pegged at **₹55,70,000.00**. These adjustments represent internal timing reclassifications between initial cash and post-launch milestones.
