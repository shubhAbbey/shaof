## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-05

### Initial Architecture & Blueprint Establishment
- **Brand Identity & Commercial Baseline:** Formally established the first-party luxury fashion brand architecture for ATEEVRA in `docs/ateevra-brand/`. ATEEVRA operates as the sole legal Seller of Record (SOR) and brand owner, delivering directly to consumers (D2C).
- **Dual Governance Invariants Preserved:** Guaranteed 100% preservation of all existing multi-vendor marketplace documentation (`docs/mvp2/m2-14/`), owner decisions (OD-01 through OD-15), marketplace policies, ADRs, and acceptance reports.
- **Owner Decision Register (ATEEVRA-OD-001 to ATEEVRA-OD-028):** Defined 28 authoritative decision areas across 21 core brand domains (Domain A through Domain U), categorized into 8 functional clusters.
- **Operations & Supply Chain SOPs:** Completed comprehensive standard operating procedures for Atelier Procurement (`ATEEVRA-OPS-PROC-001`), Inventory & Warehouse Topology (`ATEEVRA-OPS-INV-001`), Fulfilment & Express Air Logistics (`ATEEVRA-OPS-LOG-001`), and Customer Concierge Operations (`ATEEVRA-OPS-SUP-001`).
- **Research & Statutory Compliance:** Completed luxury peer benchmarking (`ATEEVRA-RES-MKT-001`) and Indian statutory regulatory framework (`ATEEVRA-RES-REG-001`) covering Legal Metrology 2021, Consumer Protection 2020, and Section 31 CGST invoicing.
- **Financial Engineering Models:** Authored First-Party Unit Economics Model (`ATEEVRA-FIN-MOD-001`), Landed Cost BOM Calculator (`ATEEVRA-FIN-CALC-001`), and Working Capital Projection Model (`ATEEVRA-FIN-CAP-001`).
- **Implementation & Acceptance:** Authored Capability Reuse & Adaptation Matrix (`ATEEVRA-ROAD-REUSE-001`), Phased Rollout Roadmap (`ATEEVRA-ROAD-PHASE-001`), Acceptance Framework (`ATEEVRA-ACC-FRAME-001`), and Professional Validation Register (`ATEEVRA-ACC-VAL-001`).
