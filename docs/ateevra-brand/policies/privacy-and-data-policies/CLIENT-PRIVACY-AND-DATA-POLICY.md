# ATEEVRA — Client Privacy and Personal Data Governance Policy

**Document ID:** `ATEEVRA-POL-PRV-001`  
**Classification:** Information Privacy & Statutory DPDP Compliance  
**Status:** PROPOSED (Pending Owner Decision)  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

---

## 1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

Discretion is paramount in the luxury sphere. ATEEVRA treats all client personal information, styling measurements, wardrobe preferences, and transaction records with absolute confidentiality under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and global privacy standards.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses and Restrictions

1. **Strict Purpose Limitation:** Client phone numbers, delivery coordinates, and measurements are processed strictly to fulfill orders, deliver express shipments, and provide requested bespoke styling advisory.
2. **Zero Third-Party Data Monetization:** ATEEVRA does not sell, rent, license, or exchange client contact details or purchase histories with any third-party marketing agency or data broker.
3. **Internal PII Masking:** Logistics personnel and warehouse packers access only masked customer phone numbers and anonymized delivery sheets to safeguard client privacy.

---

## 3. Client Rights and Erasure

Clients maintain full authority over their digital data:
- **Right to Rectify:** Modify measurements and style preferences via the Account Portal at any time.
- **Right to Forgotten:** Request complete purging of marketing records and style dossiers by notifying `privacy@ateevra.com`. Data is irrevocably deleted within 30 days, except for statutory tax invoices retained under Section 36 of the CGST Act.
