## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose of Addendum

This Addendum performs an independent evidence audit, working-capital reconciliation, and commercial reality check on the **OD-002 Owner Deliberation Dossier**.

The original dossier outlined a 500-unit debut collection requiring **₹16.01 Lakhs in production capital** and claiming over **₹65 Lakhs in GMV capacity**. While the mathematical multiplication of unit COGS by quantity was correct, this Addendum resolves critical omissions and establishes commercial transparency:
1. **Status of Sourcing Quotations:** Formally classifies all unit costs as **`UNVERIFIED — MODEL ASSUMPTIONS`** due to the absence of signed supplier contracts or pro-forma invoices in the repository.
2. **Gross Cash Outflow vs. Accounting Cost:** Identifies the GST cash gap (suppliers require GST-inclusive cash payments before ATEEVRA can offset ITC), expanding the upfront cash requirement by 5% to 12% (₹80,050 to ₹1,92,120).
3. **True Peak Working Capital:** Reconciles the ₹16.01L production figure with packaging, inbound freight, pre-launch marketing, and return reserves, demonstrating that **peak working capital required is ₹26.50 Lakhs (not ₹16.01L)**, requiring a formal contingency buffer.
4. **Realistic Sell-Through vs. GMV Claims:** Dismantles the theoretical assumption of 100% full-price sell-through, modeling realistic luxury sell-through (65% full MRP, 20% promotional floor, 15% terminal inventory/returns), and distinguishing GMV from Recognized Accounting Revenue, Cash Collections, and Contribution.
5. **Owned-Factory Breakdown:** Details the granular machinery, civil lease, and fixed payroll assumptions behind the ₹1.54 Crore CapEx and ₹27.3 Lakhs/month fixed burn.

### 2.2 Sourcing Model Cost Responsibility: FOB vs. CMT
The financial and operational liability varies significantly between Option A (FOB) and Option B (CMT):
- **Under Option A (Contract FOB — Recommended):** The atelier acts as a full-package contractor. The atelier purchases fabrics, trims, and thread to ATEEVRA's approved tech packs and specifications, absorbing fabric cutting wastage and pattern layout yield risk. ATEEVRA pays a single consolidated unit price upon delivery.
- **Under Option B (Contract CMT):** ATEEVRA must buy fabric directly from textile mills (minimum 200–500 meters per bolt), pay 100% upfront to the mill, organize transit to the CMT atelier, and bear 100% of cutting wastage and pattern defects. If the fabric has a weaving flaw, ATEEVRA bears the financial loss, not the atelier.

### 2.3 GST Cash Timing: The Upfront Cash Gap
While recoverable GST is excluded from inventory valuation under Ind AS 2, **it must be paid in cash upfront to suppliers**:
- If ateliers invoice finished garments under HSN 6203/6206 at **12.0% GST**:
  $$\text{Cash Paid to Ateliers} = ₹16,01,000.00 \times 1.12 = \mathbf{₹17,93,120.00}$$
  $$\mathbf{Upfront\ GST\ Cash\ Drain = ₹1,92,120.00}$$
- This ₹1.92 Lakhs is trapped as an input tax credit asset in GSTR-2B until customer sales generate output GST liabilities to offset it. Any working capital plan that budgets only ₹16.01L will face a **₹1.92 Lakh immediate cash deficit** upon PO delivery!

### Title Transfer & Quality Acceptance Criteria
1. **Title Transfer Invariant:** Legal title and risk of loss remain with the atelier during manufacturing and transit. Title legally transfers to ATEEVRA **only upon formal written issuance of the WH01 Inward Acceptance Certificate**.
2. **Rejection & Rework Condition:** If an inspection lot fails AQL 2.5 (more than 7 major defects in a sample of 80 garments from a 500-unit lot), the entire lot is quarantined. The atelier must either 100% inspect and rework defects at its own expense within 10 days, or forfeit Milestone 3 payment and replace the defective units.
