## 1. Legal Seller of Record Status

**ATEEVRA Luxury Enterprises Private Limited** operates as the sole legal **Seller of Record (SOR)** for all transactions originating across the brand's digital ecosystem.

1. **Direct Tax Invoicing:** Every transaction generates a direct Tax Invoice under Section 31 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017.
2. **Deactivation of Marketplace Tax Withholdings:**
   - **Section 52 TCS:** Not applicable. ATEEVRA does not operate as an e-commerce intermediary collecting tax on behalf of third-party vendors.
   - **Section 194-O TDS:** Not applicable. Gross order value constitutes direct sales revenue belonging to ATEEVRA.

---

## 2. HSN Classification & Input Tax Credit (ITC) Management

1. **HSN Code Mapping:**
   - Chapter 61 (Knitted Luxury Apparel): 12% GST
   - Chapter 62 (Woven Luxury Apparel): 12% GST
   - Chapter 42 (Leather Goods & Accessories): 18% GST
2. **Comprehensive ITC Utilization:** ATEEVRA systematically captures and offsets Input Tax Credit paid on raw textile procurement, contract atelier CMT fabrication, rigid box packaging, cloud servers, and express courier air freight against gross output GST liabilities.
