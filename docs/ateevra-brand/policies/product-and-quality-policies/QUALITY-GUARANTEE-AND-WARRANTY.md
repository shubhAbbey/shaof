## 1. Craftsmanship Guarantee & Warranty Coverage

Every ATEEVRA garment and leather good is guaranteed against manufacturing defects, structural seam failures, and hardware malfunctions for a period of **six (6) months** from the date of physical delivery.

### 1.1 Covered Defects
- Stitch unraveling or structural seam breakage under normal wearing conditions.
- Hardware failure (broken zipper sliders, loose snap buttons, sheared rivet joints).
- Unnatural dye bleeding or fabric delamination during authorized care maintenance.

### 1.2 Exclusions from Warranty
- Natural wear and tear, abrasion, surface scuffing on delicate leather, or fabric pulling caused by rough external friction.
- Shrinkage or damage resulting from failure to adhere to the garment care label (e.g., machine washing dry-clean-only silk).
- Alterations executed by unauthorized third-party tailoring services.

## 2. Atelier Repair and Replacement Protocol

1. **Concierge Intake:** Client shares photographs of the affected garment via WhatsApp Concierge.
2. **Complimentary Pickup:** ATEEVRA schedules courier pickup at zero charge to the client.
3. **Repair or Replace:** Our master tailors restore the garment to pristine condition within 7 business days. If structural restoration is unfeasible, a brand-new replacement or full credit note is issued immediately.
