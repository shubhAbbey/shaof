## 1. Ownership of Intellectual Property

All trademarks, brand heraldry, visual crests, proprietary garment patterns, tech packs, editorial photography, runway video assets, and digital typography associated with ATEEVRA are the exclusive intellectual property of **ATEEVRA Luxury Enterprises Private Limited**.

---

## 2. Anti-Counterfeiting & Grey Market Enforcement

1. **Serialization & Verification:** Every high-value garment incorporates an embedded security serial code registered in ATEEVRA's central authentication ledger.
2. **Authorized Distribution Channels:** ATEEVRA products are sold exclusively through `ateevra.com` and authorized private atelier appointments. Sales through unauthorized third-party websites, auction platforms, or flea markets are deemed counterfeit or unauthorized grey-market distributions subject to immediate civil and criminal prosecution under the Trade Marks Act, 1999 and the Copyright Act, 1957.
3. **Resale Restrictions:** Products purchased from ATEEVRA are for private personal consumption. Commercial resale without express written authorization from the Brand Director is strictly prohibited.
