## 1. Introduction and Scope of Contract

These Terms of Sale govern all purchases of garments, leather goods, and luxury accessories executed through the official ATEEVRA digital storefront (`ateevra.com`) and associated concierge ordering channels. 

All sales are executed directly by **ATEEVRA Luxury Enterprises Private Limited** (hereinafter "ATEEVRA"), acting as the sole legal **Seller of Record (SOR)**. By placing an order, the customer enters into a direct sales contract with ATEEVRA governed by the Indian Contract Act, 1872, the Sale of Goods Act, 1930, and the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

---

## 2. Order Acceptance, Availability & Pricing

1. **Order Acceptance:** The receipt of an order confirmation email acknowledges placement of the order but does not constitute formal acceptance. Acceptance and formation of the binding sales contract occur when the order is physically dispatched from our central facility and an Airway Bill (AWB) is assigned.
2. **Pricing Integrity:** All listed prices are displayed in Indian Rupees (INR) and are inclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST) as mandated by the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.
3. **Availability & Allocation:** In the event of an inadvertent inventory variance or failure at pre-dispatch quality inspection, ATEEVRA reserves the right to cancel the unfulfilled item and issue an immediate 100% refund to the original payment source.

---

## 3. Passing of Title and Risk of Loss

1. **Risk of Loss:** Risk of loss or damage to products remains with ATEEVRA throughout transit and passes to the customer only upon verified physical handover (including valid delivery OTP verification where applicable).
2. **Title of Goods:** Title and ownership of the goods pass to the customer upon full settlement of the purchase price and physical delivery.

---

## 4. Governing Law & Dispute Redressal

These terms are governed by the substantive laws of India. Any legal dispute arising out of or related to an order shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in **Bengaluru, Karnataka, India**.
