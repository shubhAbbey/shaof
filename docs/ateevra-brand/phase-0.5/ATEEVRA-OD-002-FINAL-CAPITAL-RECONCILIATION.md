### 4.3 Correction of Net Bank Collections
- In the earlier addendum, net bank collections were reported as **₹49,81,303.57**.
- **Audit Correction:** That figure was erroneously calculated by applying 2.4% to Net Revenue ($₹51,03,794 \times 0.976 = ₹49,81,303$).
- In commercial banking, the gateway collects from the **Gross Customer Payment**:
  $$\text{Gross Collections} = \mathbf{₹56,34,500.00}$$
  $$\text{Gateway Cut (2.0\% MDR + 18\% GST = 2.36\%)} = 0.0236 \times ₹56,34,500.00 = \mathbf{₹1,32,974.20}$$
  $$\mathbf{Correct\ Net\ Cash\ Deposited\ in\ Bank = ₹56,34,500.00 - ₹1,32,974.20 = ₹55,01,525.80}$$
